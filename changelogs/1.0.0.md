# Biomes of Bountiful Fares 1.0.0

## New Biomes
- Added Walnut Forests
  - Generates in warm climates, typically surrounding Savannas, Badlands, and Forests
  - Made up of Walnut and Oak Trees
  - Occasionally a Golden Walnut Tree will generate in place of a normal one
    - Has yellow leaves
    - Golden Walnut Saplings can be obtained to keep growing them
  - Black Wolves spawn here
- Added Blooming Oases
  - Desert sub biome, is relatively rare
  - Oasis springs are found here, surrounded by a range of tropical flora such as palm trees, orange "bushes", wild passion fruit, and flowers
- Added Blooming Taigas
  - Taiga sub biome
  - Made up of Plum and Flowering Spruce trees
    - Flowering Spruce Leaves create falling petal particles
  - Violet Bellflowers, Purple Posies, Alliums, and Azure Bluets generate commonly on the ground
- Each biome's generation can be enabled/disabled in the config

## New Foliage
- Added Golden Walnut Leaves and Golden Walnut Saplings
- Added Saltgrass and Dry Saltgrass
  - Saltgrass can be smelted to create Dry Saltgrass
  - Saltgrass can be found in patches on Beaches
    - Can be disabled in the config
- Added Purple Posies
- Added Flowering Spruce Leaves
